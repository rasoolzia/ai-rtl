# Make AIs RTL Privacy Policy

Make AIs RTL is a browser extension that adds right-to-left (RTL) text direction to supported AI chat interfaces.

## Data Collection

Make AIs RTL does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

The extension does not:

- Collect conversations, prompts, or AI responses
- Collect account information
- Track browsing activity
- Use analytics
- Display advertisements
- Use external servers or APIs
- Send webpage content outside the browser

## Local Storage

The extension stores only one setting locally in Chrome:

- Your RTL/LTR preference

This preference is stored using Chrome's local storage and is not transmitted to any external service.

## Website Content

Make AIs RTL runs locally in your browser on supported AI websites. It changes the visual text direction of supported messages and keeps code blocks in their natural left-to-right direction.

The extension does not modify, copy, transmit, or store your conversations.

Your conversations remain between your browser and the AI service you are using.

## Supported Platforms

The current supported platforms are:

- ChatGPT
- DeepSeek
- Claude
- Gemini
- Perplexity
- Grok

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change.

Any future changes will be reflected in this document.
